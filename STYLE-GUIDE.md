# Style guide

## Colours

| Colour | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Charcoal | `#26282C` | Marks and text on light backgrounds, and the background of avatars and banners |
| Off-white | `#EDEDED` | Marks and text on dark backgrounds |
| Orange accent | `#D8572A` | The accent square in the marks, and small highlights only |

Use only these three colours with the logos.

## Font

The wordmarks use **Schibsted Grotesk Bold**. It is free under the SIL Open Font License and available on [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Schibsted+Grotesk). In the SVG files the letters are converted to shapes, so the font does not need to be installed to show the logos.

## Light and dark

- On light backgrounds, use the `-light` files (charcoal marks).
- On dark backgrounds, use the `-dark` files (off-white marks).
- Where the background is unknown, or for avatars, use the icon on its solid charcoal square.

## Space and size

- Leave clear space around a logo at least the height of one of its squares.
- Show the icon no smaller than 16 pixels, and the lockup no narrower than 120 pixels.

## Do not

- Change the colours, or move the orange square.
- Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows or other effects.
- Rearrange the icon and the name, or set the name in another font.
- Place a logo on a busy image or a colour that hides it.
- Combine a Chroniton Labs logo with another organisation's logo into a new mark.
