Design a logo for ChronitonDB, an open source distributed database built on Apache Cassandra.

About the project:
- ChronitonDB is a community open source project. AxonOps is its steward, but the logo is the project's own identity and should not carry AxonOps branding.
- The audience is engineers and operators who run large databases. The logo should feel technical, trustworthy and calm. No mascot, no cartoon.
- A chroniton is a hypothetical particle of time. You may draw on that idea (time, continuity, a particle, an orbit) or ignore it.

Deliverables:
1. An icon mark on its own, square, for avatars and favicons. It must still read clearly at 16 and 32 pixels.
2. A horizontal lockup: the icon mark followed by the wordmark "ChronitonDB". This goes at the top of a GitHub README at about 400 pixels wide.
3. Light and dark variants of both, on transparent backgrounds. GitHub shows READMEs in both light and dark mode.
4. SVG files for everything, plus a PNG of the icon at 512 by 512.

Constraints:
- Flat design, two colours at most, no gradients, no fine detail that disappears when small. The logo may later be registered as a trademark, so it must be simple and distinctive.
- It must be original. It must not use or resemble the Apache Cassandra eye logo, the Apache feather, or any other Apache Software Foundation mark.
- Do not include the words "Apache" or "Cassandra".
- It must not resemble the logos of other databases (for example ScyllaDB, DataStax, MongoDB, Redis, CockroachDB, PostgreSQL).
- The wordmark spells the name exactly "ChronitonDB": capital C, capital D, capital B.

Show three different directions first, so I can pick one before you refine it.
